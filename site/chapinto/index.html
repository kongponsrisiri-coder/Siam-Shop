Since the early 1980s

Rumwong Thai Market, reborn

The shop opened as Surrey's first Asian supermarket and has served Guildford's Thai community for four decades, beside the Rumwong restaurant on London Road. Today it's Cha & Pinto Box — the same shelves, a new counter, and a café.
